History of English Language

CEEN621A

Unit I

Prescribed Text: The English Language by C. L. Wren
  • General Character of English
  • The Indo-European family of languages: English in the Germanic Family
  • Making of Modern English
  • Greek influence on English language
  • The Bible Translations and English Language
  • Shakespeare's contribution to English language

Unit III (A): Structure of English

Prescribed Text: The Study of Language by George Yule
  • Morphology: Derivational and inflectional affixes-bound and free morphemes
  • Word formation-simple, complex, compound words coinage-backformation, reduplication, blends, clipping, acronyms etc.

Unit III (B): Varieties of English

Standard and non-standard language, dialect, register, slang, pidgin, creole; varieties of English; language change.
