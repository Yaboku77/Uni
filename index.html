CCEN606

GENERAL ENGLISH

Literature (Prose & Poetry)
  • "The Tent" A study of the story highlighting displacement, symbolism, and the impact of natural calamities (e.g., earthquakes, floods).
  • "The Death On The Map" Analysis of central themes, motifs involving a bird, star, and borders.
  • Nissim Ezekiel "Goodbye Party for Miss Pushpa T. S." Character study, poet's use of "incorrect English," and presentation of humor.
  • Biographical Study Balaji Namkeens The entrepreneurial journey of Chandubhai Virani and brothers.
  • UNIFEST Concept and significance of the University Youth Festival.
  • Drama Drama as a universal art form and effective tool to develop communication skills.
Fundamentals of Communication
  • Introduction to Communication Defining communication, forms (oral, face-to-face, etc.), and qualities of a good communicator.
  • The Communication Process Step-by-step process and the vital role of feedback.
  • Non-Verbal & Barriers Body language (Kinesics) and identifying barriers to effective communication.
Soft Skills & Career Preparation
  • Overview of Soft Skills Relating to spoken/written communication and maintaining a positive attitude.
  • Group Discussions & Interviews Objectives, structure of GDs (size 5-10 members), and different types of interviews.
  • Professional Development Leadership qualities, Critical Thinking, and Stress Management techniques.
CEEN617

HISTORY OF ENGLISH LITERATURE (1890-1960)

Literary Movements & Context
  • Impact of social, political, and economic changes on 20th-century English literature
  • Characteristics of Movement Poetry
  • Development of the Psychological Novel (and its narrative techniques)
  • Revival of Poetic Drama in the 20th century
  • The "Angry Young Man" movement
Poetry
  • T.S. Eliot "To the Indian Who Died in Africa" Theme of exile and identity.
  • W.B. Yeats "The Second Coming" Apocalyptic imagery, symbolism of the rough beast.
  • Philip Larkin "Church Going" Significance of the church as a symbol.
  • W.H. Auden "The Unknown Citizen" Critique of modern bureaucracy, central irony.
Drama
  • John Osborne Look Back in Anger Themes of anger/disillusionment, social issues, characters of Jimmy & Alison Porter, the "bear and squirrel game" symbolism.

CEEN619

READING NON-FICTIONAL PROSE

Prose Works
  • V.S. Naipaul India: A Wounded Civilization Post-colonial state of India, The Emergency, Naipaul's self-loathing/heritage struggle, criticism of Indian society and civilization.

CEEN6S-22-A

AMERICAN LITERATURE

Literary Movements & Concepts
  • Impact of Modernism and Postmodernism How these movements break from traditional forms and reflect the changing worldviews of the time.
  • Naturalism Definition and context within American literature.
  • Realism Key characteristics in American literary history.
Poetry
  • Wallace Stevens "Sunday Morning" Themes, poetic techniques, Modernist concerns, and the associated literary movement.
  • Robert Frost "Mending Wall" Theme of isolation and boundaries; context as a 20th-century poem.
  • Sylvia Plath "Lady Lazarus" Reading as a subversive poem; themes; references to the Gospel (e.g., John).
  • Langston Hughes "The Negro Mother" The representation of African-American identity.
Novel & Drama
  • Ernest Hemingway The Old Man and the Sea Themes of victory, defeat, struggle, and existentialism. Symbolism of the marlin and the struggle with nature. (Context: Publication year and awareness of author's other works like *For Whom the Bell Tolls*).
  • Tennessee Williams The Glass Menagerie Justification of the title, analysis of the central conflict, study of progressive characters, and reading the text as autobiographical.
